Build Assumptions

Boards are estimated as 5/8 in. x 5-1/2 in. dog-ear pickets with 5.5 in. coverage per board.

Posts are 4x4x8 ground-contact pressure-treated posts set with two 60 lb concrete bags each.

Rails are kiln-dried 2x4x8 boards: two rails for 4-5 ft fence, three rails for 6 ft fence.

Rails use two brackets per rail bay. Gate hardware is counted per gate with hinge/latch set and anti-sag bracing.
